# Work inventory

Raw, exhaustive list of past work, to be turned into accomplishments. **List first, judge later**: add every row, however small. Merging and cutting happen during capture.

## Legend

**Buckets** (sort each row by *who started it?*):
1. **Assigned work**: stories, features and tasks from a board or manager, with the bugs found in them listed under each
2. **Production and customer issues**: defects and incidents raised by customers, support or monitoring
3. **Self-initiated work**: things nobody asked for (tools, docs, process fixes, investigations)
4. **Help given to others**: answering questions, unblocking teammates, onboarding, reviews, helping a lead refine work

**IDs**: `A1`, with its bugs `A1-B1`, `A1-B2`…; `P1`…; `S1`…; `H1`…; recognition `R1`…

**When**: `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY-MM – YYYY-MM`. Leave blank if unknown, never guess.

**Kind of work** (one or more): `Technical` (programming, debugging, reverse engineering) · `Documentation` (release notes, guides, onboarding docs, other technical writing) · `Behavioural:<tag>` where tag is `ownership`, `collaboration`, `leadership`, `innovation` or `mentoring`

**Impact markers**: `[have]` known, with source · `[todo]` to collect · `[n/a]` can't be obtained (becomes a gap, never an estimate) · `[ai]` suggested by the agent, not yet collected

**Confidentiality**: no client names, people's names, internal URLs, code or confidential figures. Describe tickets by type, month and topic, not by link.
