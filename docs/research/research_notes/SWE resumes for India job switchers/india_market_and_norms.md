# India resume norms and hiring market for services-to-product SWE switchers (2-3 YOE)

Researcher notes. Access date for all URLs: 2026-11-07 unless stated. Confidence labels: HIGH = primary official/academic document read directly; MEDIUM = primary source seen only through press or search summaries, or industry-body data; LOW = practitioner community, aggregator, or self-reported.

Source-policy note: Sources that sell services to job seekers were excluded. Several search results came from resume builders or interview-prep sellers (resumegyani.in, placementpreparation.io, lets-code.co.in, bosscoderacademy.com, interviewbit.com, naukri.com/code360, geeksforgeeks, shashiworks.com, unboxcareer.com, airtribe.live). None are used as evidence. Naukri JobSpeak is used only as labour-market data from a job-board operator (Info Edge), labelled as such. It is not used for resume advice.

## 1. India resume conventions: what to include and exclude

### Takeaway
The one primary university source read in full (IIT Madras CDC, 2025-26) bans photos and all contact or personal details on placement-portal resumes. It requires exact CGPA plus 10th/12th scores, and every claim must be backed by proof. Legacy Indian "biodata" fields (DOB, father's name, marital status, declaration) appear in older templates from lower-tier colleges and on job boards. No authoritative current source recommends them. DPDP Act 2023 data-minimisation principles point the same way, but the Act regulates employers, not what candidates may write.

### Cited Findings
- **IIT Madras Career Development Cell, "Internship Resume Guidelines 2025-26" (PDF, 10 pp.)** (HIGH; read in full):
  - "Name, Roll Number should be present on an A4 resume however Contact information, including, but not limited to, mobile number, address, email address should NOT be mentioned in a resume."
  - "No pictures or any logo apart from the IIT-M logo should be present in the resume."
  - LinkedIn, Google Scholar, GitHub and Codeforces links are allowed.
  - "Rounding of CGPA or any scores is strictly prohibited… 7.99 is NOT 8." CGPA is mandatory on every resume. Scanned 10th and 12th mark sheets are required as proof in the master resume, and "intermediate (11th+12th)" percentage must not be presented as the 12th percentage.
  - Each claim (internships, projects, PORs, startups, certifications, online courses) needs a proof document. Personal projects need "a link to the report or code".
  - Department rank may be stated only if top 5.
  - Students are warned not to copy points from archived resumes because "it will be evident to the recruiter".
  - Source: [IIT Madras CDC, Internship Resume Guidelines 2025-26](https://internship.iitm.ac.in/downloads/Internship_Resume_Guidelines_2025-26.pdf)
  - Context: these rules apply to campus portal resumes, where the institute anonymises contact details and verifies claims. They are not lateral-hire rules. Still, they show that the top Indian placement ecosystem uses no photo, no personal details, exact GPA, and verifiable claims.
- Other college templates differ. Search snippets showed a Hansraj College internship-cell template with "Gender | Age | Phone Number | Email ID" in its header, and an LNJ Pitampura guide saying DOB, family details and marital status are not required. Only the snippets were seen, so both are LOW. Sources: [LNJ guide](https://www.lnjpitchapra.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/file_5e81a560cca4f.docx); [Gurugram University T&P resume page](https://gurugramuniversity.ac.in/trainingandplacement/resume.php)
- A Naukri company-hosted resume template includes a date-of-birth field (snippet only, LOW). This suggests job-board templates still carry legacy fields. — [Naukri resume-template.doc](https://company.naukri.com/landing-page/snp/resume-template.doc)
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025:**
  - MeitY notified the DPDP Rules on 13 Nov 2025, with obligations phased in. Reports put compliance deadlines between Nov 2026 and May 2027. — [SCC Online blog, 14 Nov 2025](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/11/14/meity-notified-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025/); [DSCI Insight Brief, 17 Nov 2025](https://www.dsci.in/resource/content/insight-brief-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025); [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/govt-notifies-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-rollout-in-phases-specified-obligations-for-data-fiduciaries)
  - Section 7(i) lists processing "for the purposes of employment or those related to safeguarding the employer from loss or liability" as a legitimate use that does not need consent. Law-firm commentary says it is unsettled whether this covers job applicants who are not yet employees, and whether rejected candidates' data must be deleted. Duties under Section 8, including erasure once the purpose ends, still apply. — [Bar & Bench, "Navigating Legitimate Use Exemption for Employee Data"](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/navigating-legitimate-use-exemption-employee-data-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023); [Bar & Bench, "From Consent to Compliance" (Kripalani & Sarkar)](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/from-consent-to-compliance-navigating-the-implications-of-dpdp-act-on-employment-practices); [Mondaq, "Is consent required to process employees' personal data under the DPDP Act"](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1755320/is-consent-required-to-process-employees-personal-data-under-the-dpdp-act)
  - Data minimisation and purpose limitation are described as core principles of the Act. — [Mondaq, "DPDP Rules 2025 Notified"](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1708164/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified)
  - One compliance wiki says the employment-related provisions take effect 13 May 2027, 18 months after publication. This is not verified against the Gazette (LOW). — [DPDP Wiki, Section 7](https://dpdp.myndsolution.com/wiki/act/section-7-certain-legitimate-uses/)

### Inferences
- For a lateral candidate, the evidence supports leaving out the photo, DOB, gender, marital status, father's name, religion, full postal address and the "I hereby declare…" line. Contact details (phone, email, city, LinkedIn, GitHub) are needed because there is no portal to anonymise them, unlike the IIT-M campus case. City-level location is useful because Indian postings are city-specific.
- The DPDP Act binds employers (data fiduciaries), not candidates. Its minimisation logic means employers have little legitimate reason to collect DOB or marital status at the resume stage. That is an argument for omitting these fields, not a legal prohibition.
- CGPA and percentages carry weight in Indian screening; the IIT-M rules treat them as mandatory and verified. For someone with 2-3 YOE, listing an exact degree CGPA (or percentage) seems low-risk and common, and BGV will verify it. 10th/12th scores matter less for laterals. No source covering laterals was found on this.
- The emphasis on verifiable claims (proof for every item, code links for projects) matches Indian employers' heavy use of background verification. Unverifiable or inflated claims are risky.

### Gaps
- No lateral-hire resume guide from an Indian university career cell (IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIMs, NITs, IIITs) could be retrieved. IIT Delhi and IIT Bombay guides appear only as Scribd copies, which were not used. No public IIM guidance was found.
- No Indian product company or GCC careers page was found that states a policy on photo, DOB, CTC on the resume, or page length.
- No authoritative source was found on CTC/expected CTC or notice period on resumes. In practice these are collected in application forms and recruiter calls (not verified). Recommendation: not on the resume. This is an inference only.
- One page vs two pages: no authoritative Indian source found for laterals. IIT-M specifies one A4 page for campus resumes ("an A4 resume").
- Equal-opportunity law: I did not retrieve statutory text. From background knowledge, unverified and needing confirmation via indiacode.nic.in: Section 9 of the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019 bars discrimination in employment including recruitment, and the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 has non-discrimination duties, strongest for government establishments. India has no general private-sector ban on asking age or marital status comparable to US EEOC guidance. I found no Indian government guidance on what personal details resumes should contain.

## 2. Labour-market context: GCCs, product and startup hiring vs IT services (2024-2026)

### Takeaway
Industry-body data (NASSCOM, NASSCOM-Zinnov) show India's GCCs at about 2,100+ firms and 2.36M employees (FY26), growing faster than the overall tech sector. Total tech headcount is about 5.95M and grows about 2-2.5% a year. Job-board data (Naukri, Nov 2025) show GCC postings +18% YoY against +14% for IT/software services, with AI/ML +72%. Most of these figures come from bodies whose members gain from a "growth" narrative.

### Inferences
- The structural trend supports the switch strategy: GCCs and product/unicorn firms are growing faster than IT services, while total sector headcount is only growing about 2-2.5% a year. That puts the most new demand at the employers the user is targeting. Competition is also heavy, because many services engineers are trying the same move.
- Early-career (0-3 yrs) postings on Naukri were growing in late 2025, but that band mixes freshers with 2-3 YOE laterals.
- AI/ML demand (+72%) suggests that AI-adjacent work experience or projects are a strong signal in 2025-26, though the base effect is unknown.

### Gaps
- No MeitY, RBI or PLFS statistic isolating software-engineer employment or GCC/product hiring was found in this pass. PLFS does not break out IT services vs product. Independent government numbers on GCC size do not appear to exist; all GCC counts trace back to NASSCOM-Zinnov or other consultancies.
- No reliable data on startup tech hiring volumes in 2025-26. One 2025 commentary claimed startup hiring fell to "low single digit" growth (secondary, not verified).
- The full NASSCOM-Zinnov FY26 GCC report text was not accessible.

## 3. How product companies and GCCs perceive IT-services candidates; skill gaps and signals

### Takeaway
I found no peer-reviewed study that directly measures how Indian product firms or GCCs evaluate ex-services engineers. Academic work describes services work as producing "generic programmers" and fragmented coding tasks, and documents that "merit" screening in Indian IT is socially loaded. Employer interview processes (DSA, machine coding, LLD/HLD design, hiring-manager rounds), as shown in postings and LOW-confidence candidate reports, reveal the signals that matter: problem solving, design, ownership, and product-company experience.

### Cited Findings
- **Ethnography of an Indian IT services firm (AMCIS 2021, SIG LEAD).** Traditional services work turned engineers into "generic programmers", and mobility constraints remain for moving into emerging-technology roles. Based on four months' ethnography at one IT services organisation. (MEDIUM; abstract-level) — [AIS eLibrary, AMCIS 2021 Proceedings, "IS Leadership and the IT Profession (SIG LEAD)"](https://aisel.aisnet.org/amcis2021/is_leadership/sig_lead/2)
- **Daxini, S. (2025), "Graduates in India's Silicon Valley: Who Gains in the Knowledge Economy?"**, in *Higher Education and Work in the Knowledge Economy*, Springer, ch. 12, pp. 273-293, DOI 10.1007/978-3-031-80618-6_12. Studies early-career engineers and data scientists at MNCs and startups in Bangalore. It asks whether their work is intellectually stimulating R&D or "outsourced, fragmented coding tasks". The author notes the findings are subjective. (MEDIUM; abstract only) — [EconPapers record](https://econpapers.repec.org/bookchap/sprsprchp/978-3-031-80618-6_5f12.htm)
- **Upadhya, C. (2007), "Employment, Exclusion and 'Merit' in the Indian IT Industry"**, *Economic & Political Weekly* (reportedly 19 May 2007). Reprinted in S. Deshpande (ed.), *The Problem of Caste: Essays from EPW*, pp. 141-151. Draws on Bangalore IT-workforce data to critique the ideology of "merit" in hiring, linking it to unequal access to elite education. (MEDIUM; bibliographic record and secondary summary only; older data) — [NIAS repository record](https://cms.nias.res.in/node/2459)
- **Fernandez, M. (2017/2018), *The New Frontier: Merit vs. Caste in the Indian IT Sector*, OUP.** Argues caste inequality is "layered on socially constructed 'pure merit'" in IT hiring. (MEDIUM) — [Santa Clara Univ. Scholar Commons](https://scholarcommons.scu.edu/faculty_books/386)
- **Commander, Chanda, Kangasniemi & Winters (2008), "The Consequences of Globalisation: India's Software Industry and Cross-Border Labour Mobility", *The World Economy* 31(2):187-211.** A firm survey on cross-border mobility. It concerns mobility abroad, not services-to-product moves (older data; tangential). — [EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:worlde:v:31:y:2008:i:2:p:187-211); [IIMB record](https://research.iimb.ac.in/fac_pubs/942)
- **Krishnan, R.T. & Prabhu, G.N. (c. 2002), "Innovation in the Indian Information Technology Industry: A Study of the Software Product Development Process"** (IIMB). Case studies of six product-development projects. It frames product work as distinct from services (older). — [IIMB record](https://research.iimb.ac.in/fac_pubs/1243)
- **Employer/posting signals** (LOW-MEDIUM; aggregator copies of postings):
  - One Flipkart SDE-2 listing lists problem solving, algorithms, Java, and system design (LLD and HLD), and says "product base startup company experience is a must". Flipkart's own description, quoted in listings: SDE-2s "create features based on product requirements", with design, coding and mentoring responsibilities. — [weekday.works Flipkart SDE-2 listing](https://jobs.weekday.works/flipkart-software-development-engineer-ii-(sde-2)); [Grapevine listing](https://www.grapevine.in/tal/jobs/aa4b0f60-6bc1-4843-8be1-5f9ee2eabc7d)
  - Candidate-reported Flipkart SDE-2 process: machine coding, DSA, a design round of about one hour (LLD plus some HLD), and a hiring-manager round. "At SDE-2 level, design is the differentiator." (LOW; self-reported) — [LeetCode Discuss, Flipkart SDE-2 interview experience, Jan 2026](https://leetcode.com/discuss/post/8455829/flipkart-sde-2-interview-experience-jan-o8ngx/)
- **Practitioner community (LOW):**
  - The r/developersIndia wiki page on service-to-product switching (advice from u/funnythrone) recommends: implement basic data structures, understand Big-O, practise on HackerRank/HackerEarth hiring challenges, and build an end-to-end personal project.
  - It also says "A person with above average skill and excellent communication skill will be more preferable" and that interviews are subjective.
  - Source: [devsindia wiki, "Switch from Service based to product based organizations"](https://devsindia-wiki.netlify.app/faqs/switching-jobs/service-based-to-product-based)
- **Skill-shortage study:** "An Analysis of the Skill Shortage Problems in Indian IT Companies", *Social Sciences* (MDPI) 7(9):159, 2018. Seen only as a title, not read. — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/gam/jscscx/v7y2018i9p159-d169824.html)

### Inferences
- Taken together, the services resume's weakness is perceived scope: maintenance and ticket work, client-dictated tasks, and older stacks. That fits the academic "generic programmer" and "fragmented tasks" framing and postings that ask for "product experience". The resume should foreground ownership of features or modules, measurable impact (latency, scale, users, cost, incidents), design decisions, and modern-stack keywords that match the JD. Generic project and client names add little.
- Evaluation runs through DSA, machine coding and design rounds, so resume signals that predict those (competitive programming ratings, GitHub with substantial code, design write-ups) are likely useful for services candidates without a product brand. This is an inference; no study confirms predictive validity.
- The "merit" literature suggests that college-tier and pedigree screening is real in Indian IT hiring. Candidates from non-elite colleges benefit more from verifiable skill signals and referrals.

### Gaps
- No IIM, ISB or IIT working paper was found that empirically compares services and product career trajectories or recruiter perception of services resumes. This is a genuine literature gap, not only a search limit (moderate confidence).
- No employer-published guidance from Indian product firms or GCCs addresses candidates from IT services directly.
- Krishna & Brihmadesam (2006, EPW, "What Does It Take to Become a Software Professional?") was seen only as a citation and not read.

## 4. Leveling: SDE-1 vs SDE-2, and where 2-3 YOE maps

### Takeaway
At Amazon India, SDE II postings typically require "3+ years of non-internship professional software development experience", with some adding "2+ years of design or architecture experience". So 2-3 YOE usually sits at the SDE-1/SDE-2 boundary: below 3 years generally points to SDE-1 at Amazon-style ladders. Flipkart and Indian startups sometimes open SDE-2 from about 2-2.5 years. SDE-2 readiness is signalled by design ownership, independently delivered features, and mentoring.

### Cited Findings
- **Amazon India SDE II basic qualifications:** most commonly "3+ years of non-internship professional software development experience". Some postings add "2+ years of non-internship design or architecture (design patterns, reliability and scaling) of new and existing systems experience". One Fees & Billing posting inconsistently shows 1+ years in one section and 3+ in another. (MEDIUM; aggregator copies of amazon.jobs postings, which may be inactive) — [Jointaro mirror of Amazon SDE II posting](https://www.jointaro.com/jobs/amazon/software-development-engineer-ii-981d2be4); [apna.co Amazon India SDE II listing](https://apna.co/job/bengaluru/software-dev-engineer-ii-1921035727); [Baxter/DE Jobs Fees & Billing SDE II](https://baxter.dejobs.org/bengaluru-ind/software-development-engineer-ii-fees-billing-awd/1FB9819B781F4797A6280454F35D0DC2/job/)
- **Flipkart SDE-2:** one listing gives about 2.5-5 years excluding internships, and another board lists a minimum of 2 years. Grapevine salary data groups SDE-2 under 2-4 and 4-6 years. (LOW; aggregators) — [weekday.works Flipkart SDE-2](https://jobs.weekday.works/flipkart-software-development-engineer-ii-(sde-2)); [Grapevine](https://www.grapevine.in/tal/jobs/aa4b0f60-6bc1-4843-8be1-5f9ee2eabc7d); [foundit Flipkart SDE 2 listing](https://www.foundit.in/job/sde-2-flipkart-bengaluru-bangalore-47552048)
- SDE-2 role content at Flipkart, per quoted descriptions: building features from product requirements, design, coding and mentoring. The design round (LLD and HLD) is the reported differentiator (LOW; see Section 3). — [LeetCode Discuss](https://leetcode.com/discuss/post/8455829/flipkart-sde-2-interview-experience-jan-o8ngx/)

### Inferences
- For a services engineer with 2-3 YOE, SDE-1 is the realistic default at Amazon-ladder firms (Amazon, and many GCCs that copy its 3+ year bar). SDE-2 is plausible at startups and some unicorns that open from 2-2.5 years, especially with strong design rounds. Services years may be discounted if the work lacked design ownership. This is a widely held practitioner belief, but I found no direct source.
- Resume signals for SDE-2 readiness, inferred from the posting requirements: owning the design of a component or service end to end, reliability and scaling decisions, leading a feature from requirements to production, mentoring or reviewing juniors, and on-call or incident ownership.

### Gaps
- No public, employer-published India career-ladder document (for example, an engineering-levels page from Flipkart, Swiggy, Razorpay or a GCC) was found. Commercial salary/level sites (levels.fyi, AmbitionBox) were not consulted because of the source policy and limited time. If used, they should be labelled.
- Microsoft India (SDE vs SDE II), Google (L3/L4) and GCC title mappings were not verified in this pass.

## 5. Channels: referrals, LinkedIn, Naukri, Instahyre/Cutshort, recruiters

### Takeaway
Referrals are the channel Indian recruiters most value. LinkedIn's India Recruiting Trends surveys found 55% (2016) and then 63% (2017) of talent leaders naming referrals as the top source of quality hires, but these data are old. Naukri is the dominant job board, with about 1 lakh clients and over 70 lakh new job mandates a year per its own report, and recruiters search its resume database directly. I found no authoritative evidence on how Naukri parses resumes.

### Cited Findings
- **LinkedIn India Recruiting Trends:** 55% (2016) and 63% (2017) of Indian talent leaders named employee referrals as the most important source of quality hires. A press account said at least 65% of Indian recruiters used referral programmes, versus about 32% in China and Australia. (MEDIUM for the survey, LOW for the press restatement; dated 2016-2017) — [Business Insider India, LinkedIn survey](https://www.businessinsider.in/Majority-of-talent-leaders-in-India-want-a-push-in-hiring-budgets-states-LinkedIn-survey/articleshow/55559164.cms); [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/?p=12311)
- An older (2011) MyHiringClub survey found about 32% of IT/ITES hiring in H1 of that fiscal came via referrals. (LOW; old) — [Business Standard, 2011](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/30-hiring-through-employee-referrals-experts-111083000161_1.html)
- **Naukri self-description (JobSpeak Nov 2025):** the JobSpeak index is built from "new job listings and recruiter searches on Naukri.com's resume database". Naukri reports about 30 lakh daily job applications, about 1 lakh clients, about 1 crore active app installs and about 70 lakh new job mandates a year, and claims the highest market share among Indian job sites. (Self-reported, so MEDIUM) — [Naukri JobSpeak Nov 2025](https://www.infoedge.in/pdfs/News_Events_pdfs/Naukri-Jobspeak-Nov-2025.pdf)
- The r/developersIndia wiki recommends HackerRank/HackerEarth hiring challenges as a route to interview calls. (LOW) — [devsindia wiki](https://devsindia-wiki.netlify.app/faqs/switching-jobs/service-based-to-product-based)

### Inferences
- Recruiters actively search Naukri's database, which is a search over profile fields and keywords. So for Naukri, the profile (headline, key skills, experience fields, current/expected CTC and notice period fields) arguably matters as much as the uploaded resume. CTC and notice period belong in those platform fields rather than in the resume PDF. This is an inference; I found no Naukri documentation on parsing.
- Given the weight of referrals, the resume should be quick to forward: one clean PDF that a referrer can scan, with a JD-matched summary line.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 data on the referral share of tech hires in India.
- No data found on Instahyre, Cutshort or Wellfound usage or effectiveness. No official documentation on Naukri or LinkedIn resume parsing or ATS behaviour. Most "ATS" claims online come from resume-builder vendors excluded by the source policy.
- Could not verify how often recruiters reach out unsolicited (LinkedIn/Naukri InMail) for 2-3 YOE services engineers.
